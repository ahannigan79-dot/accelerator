1. PURPOSE
This policy sets out how goods and services are to be procured across all Krishna Industries locations (Pune Works, Coimbatore Plant, Chakan warehouse, Corporate Office Mumbai) to ensure value for money, proper authorisation and audit trail.

2. SCOPE
Applies to all purchases of raw material, consumables, capital items, services and contract labour. Excludes: payroll, statutory payments, inter-company transfers (see KI-FIN-011), and "petty cash purchases below Rs. 5,000" (see 7.4).

3. DEFINITIONS
PR = Purchase Requisition (raised in SAP ME51N, or on the paper form KI-F-22 at Coimbatore)
PO = Purchase Order
RFQ = Request for Quotation
DoA = Delegation of Authority (Annexure A)
MRM = Monthly Review Meeting

4. PROCESS OVERVIEW
4.1 Requester raises PR with material code, qty, required date, cost centre and justification.
4.2 PR is released by the Cost Centre owner (SAP release strategy). PRs above Rs 2,00,000 also require HOD release.
4.3 Purchase Dept converts released PR to RFQ. Minimum THREE quotations required for any purchase above Rs 50,000 (see also 6.2 — single source exceptions).
4.4 Comparative statement (CS) prepared by Buyer on template KI-F-31 and approved per DoA.
4.5 PO created in SAP (ME21N) and released per Annexure A release strategy.
4.6 PO sent to vendor. Vendor acknowledgement to be filed.
4.7 Goods receipt (MIGO) by Stores against PO. Quality inspection for raw material as per KI-QA-009.
4.8 Invoice verification (MIRO) by Accounts — 3-way match (PO / GRN / Invoice). Tolerance 2% on qty, 0% on price.
4.9 Payment as per vendor payment terms (standard 60 days from invoice date; MSME vendors 45 days as per MSMED Act).

5. DELEGATION OF AUTHORITY (summary — full matrix in Annexure A)
PO value (INR)                Approver
up to 1,00,000                Purchase Manager
1,00,001 – 5,00,000           Plant Head / Functional Head
5,00,001 – 25,00,000          Plant Head + CFO
above 25,00,000               CFO + MD
Capital items (any value)     as above PLUS Capex Committee approval (KI-FIN-014)

NOTE: values are per PO. Splitting of POs to avoid approval limits is strictly prohibited (see Internal Audit observation 2019/07).

6. VENDOR MANAGEMENT
6.1 Only vendors in the Approved Vendor List (AVL) may be issued POs. New vendors to complete KI-F-40 (vendor registration) with GST certificate, PAN, cancelled cheque, MSME certificate if applicable. Vendor Master created by Purchase Dept after Finance verifies bank details.
6.2 Single-source purchase permitted only where (a) OEM spare, (b) proprietary item, (c) emergency breakdown — in each case Plant Head to sign the single-source justification form KI-F-33.
6.3 Vendor performance to be reviewed quarterly (this is not currently happening — R.M. note in margin)

7. EXCEPTIONS
7.1 Emergency / breakdown purchases: Plant Head may authorise verbally; PO to be regularised within 3 working days.
7.2 Repeat orders against a valid rate contract do not require fresh quotations.
7.3 Services under Rs 25,000 may be procured on a Work Order instead of PO.
7.4 Petty cash purchases below Rs 5,000 per transaction, max Rs 25,000 per month per location.

8. RECORDS
All PR, RFQ, CS, PO, GRN and invoice records to be retained for 8 years. SAP is the system of record for Pune and Mumbai. Coimbatore operates on Tally until SAP rollout (planned FY22-23 — DID NOT HAPPEN, still on Tally as of writing this note - Suresh).

Release group 01 (Pune), 02 (Mumbai). Release codes: PM = Purchase Mgr, PH = Plant Head, FH = Functional Head, CF = CFO, MD = MD.
